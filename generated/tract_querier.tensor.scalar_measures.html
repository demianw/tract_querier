tract_querier.tensor.scalar_measures.fractional_anisotropy(tensor_array)[source]¶

Fractional Anisotropy (Basser et al.) measure computed as

FA = \sqrt{\frac {3(mn \cdot I - T) : (mn \cdot I - T)} {2(T : T)}} mn = Tr(T) / 3

Volume Fraction (Basser et al.) measure computed as:

VF = 1 - \frac{|T|} {(Tr(T) / 3.)^3}
